🛒 SmartShelf: Edge-Cloud AI for Zero-Waste Kitchens

SmartShelf is an intelligent food management system designed to tackle household food waste—a major contributor to global methane emissions. By combining low-cost IoT hardware with a MERN-stack dashboard and Agentic AI, SmartShelf predicts food spoilage in real-time and provides actionable solutions to ensure no ingredient goes to waste.
